2×2×2 cube — one matrix + one perspective divide

Each frame: every point goes through P · T · R (rotation, then translate away from the camera, then perspective projection) as a single 4×4 matrix multiply. That gives clip-space (x, y, z, w). Then, separately, x/w and y/w is computed — the one non-matrix step — to land in −1..1 space before mapping to pixels.
